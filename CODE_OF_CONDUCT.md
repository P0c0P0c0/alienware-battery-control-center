# Code of Conduct

Treat other contributors with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback and accept good-faith corrections. Protect personal information and make room for different experience levels. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct and disclosure of personal information are unacceptable.

Report incidents privately to a project maintainer through an established contact. Maintain confidentiality where possible. Maintainers may remove harmful material, restrict participation or ask for corrective action. A permanent reporting contact will be documented before public publication.
